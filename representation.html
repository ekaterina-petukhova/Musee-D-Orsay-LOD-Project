Knowledge Representation

The technical realization of the conceptual model: a TEI/XML encoding of the source text with entity annotation, and the RDF/Turtle knowledge graph produced from it.

The full narrative text is encoded in TEI, with real-world entities marked up in listPerson, listPlace, listOrg, listEvent and listBibl, each reconciled to authority files via idno. Inline references (persName, placeName, term, rs, quote) tag every mention in the running text.

The TEI document is transformed into an RDF graph reusing Schema.org, FOAF, SKOS, Dublin Core Terms and BIBO, with owl:sameAs links out to Wikidata, VIAF, ISNI, GeoNames and the Library of Congress Subject Headings.
